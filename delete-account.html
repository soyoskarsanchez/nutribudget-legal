Delete your NutriBudget account

You can permanently delete your account and all your data in two ways:

Option 1 — From the app (immediate)

If you have the app installed and are signed in:

  1. Go to the Home screen
  2. Tap the "Delete my account" button (next to "Sign out")
  3. Confirm twice

Your account and all your data are erased instantly and permanently.

Option 2 — Without the app installed

If you no longer have the app installed or cannot access your account, write to us from the same email address you signed up with, saying that you want to delete your NutriBudget account:

Write to delete my account
Response time: we process requests received by email within a maximum of 30 days. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

What is deleted?

The following is permanently erased:

  • Your profile (country, age, weight, height, goal, etc.)
  • Your pantry
  • Your saved budgets
  • Your meal plan
  • Your login account (email and password)
