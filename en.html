1. Privacy policy

At NutriBudget we take your privacy seriously. This notice explains what information we collect, what we use it for, and what we do not do with it.

What information do we collect?

When you use NutriBudget, we may collect:

  • Information you give us directly: your country of residence, the language of your recipes (English or Spanish), gender, age, weight, height, physical activity level, goal (lose weight, maintain, or build muscle), and optionally a name or nickname to personalize your greeting inside the app.
  • Account information: if you sign up, your email address. If you use guest mode, an anonymous account is created without an email address or password.
  • App usage information: the budgets you set, the ingredients you mark as part of your pantry, and the recipes and meal plans you create or save.
  • Advertising technical data: because we use Google AdMob to show ads, Google may collect device identifiers, an approximate IP address, and ad interaction data, in accordance with Google's own privacy policies.

What do we use this information for?

  • To calculate your estimated calorie and macronutrient needs
  • To show you recipes and meal plans that fit your budget
  • To save your progress (pantry, weekly budget, meal plan) so you do not have to enter it again every time you open the app
  • To personalize the greeting inside the app with your name or nickname, if you provided one
  • To show ads through Google AdMob, which help keep the app free

Where is your information stored?

Your data is stored securely with Supabase, a database provider with access controls (Row Level Security) that ensure only you can view and modify your own information. The Supabase servers we use are located in the United States. If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, this involves an international transfer of your data outside the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom; Supabase relies on standard contractual clauses and other safeguards to protect your information in accordance with applicable law.

Legal basis for processing

Where data protection laws such as the GDPR or the UK GDPR apply, we process your data on the basis of: your consent (when you sign up and accept these terms), the need to provide the service you ask for (calculating your recommendations and saving your progress), and our legitimate interest in keeping the app running for free through advertising.

Do we share your information with third parties?

We do not sell your personal information to anyone. The only "sharing" of data happens with:

  • Supabase, as the infrastructure provider that stores your data securely (it acts as a processor, not as the owner of your data)
  • Google AdMob, for delivering ads inside the app, in accordance with its own privacy and advertising policies. You can read how Google handles this data at policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Your ad choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing ads, and you can review or change that choice at any time from the "Legal" screen inside the app ("Manage my ad consent"). On Android you can also limit ad personalization in your device's Google settings.

Guest mode

If you use the app as a guest, your data is still saved (so the app can work), but under an anonymous account with no contact details. If you sign out without signing up with an email and password, you permanently lose access to that data, because an anonymous account cannot be recovered.

Your rights

You can edit or correct your personal data at any time from the "Edit my profile" screen inside the app. You can permanently and immediately delete your account and all your data with the "Delete my account" button inside the app, or by writing to: contacto.nutribudget@gmail.com. You can also write to that address to ask for access to, or a copy of, the personal information we hold about you.

If you are in the European Union, in addition to the rights above, you have the right to: request a copy of your data (portability), object to the processing of your data, request that we restrict its use, and withdraw your consent at any time. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) or with the supervisory authority of your country if you believe we have not handled your data properly.

If you are in the United Kingdom, you have similar rights under UK data protection law and may lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). If you are in Canada, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada. If you are in the United States, including California, you may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by writing to the address above.

How long do we keep your data?

We keep your data while your account is active. If you delete your account, your data is permanently and immediately erased from our systems.

Children

NutriBudget is not directed to children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under that age.

Changes to this notice

We may update this privacy notice from time to time. We will let you know about important changes through the app.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy notice, write to us at: contacto.nutribudget@gmail.com

3. Medical and nutrition disclaimer

NutriBudget is NOT a professional medical or nutrition advice service.

The information, calorie and macronutrient calculations (protein, carbohydrates, fat) and recipe recommendations offered by this app are approximate estimates based on general formulas (such as the basal metabolic rate calculation) and on the information you provide (weight, height, age, gender, activity level, goal).

These calculations:

  • Do not replace a consultation with a licensed doctor, registered dietitian or other qualified health professional
  • Do not take into account pre-existing medical conditions, allergies, food intolerances, pregnancy, breastfeeding, or medications you may be taking
  • May not be suitable for people with special nutritional needs

We recommend consulting a health professional before making significant changes to your diet, especially if you have a medical condition, are under treatment, are pregnant, are breastfeeding, or are a minor.

You use this information at your own risk and responsibility.

4. Price disclaimer

The ingredient and recipe prices shown by NutriBudget are market estimates whose only purpose is to help you plan your budget approximately.

These prices:

  • May not match the actual prices at the store where you shop
  • Vary by the specific region within your country, the type of store (local market, supermarket, convenience store), the season, and current promotions
  • Are not updated in real time — they are based on a reference catalog that is reviewed periodically, not on a direct connection to store prices

NutriBudget does not guarantee the accuracy of these prices and is not responsible for differences between the estimated cost inside the app and the actual cost at the time of your purchase.
